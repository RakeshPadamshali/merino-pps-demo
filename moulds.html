Bed Family Status Search
One set, one press at a time. A set is sized to one bed length and can only be mounted on a press of that bed (and, for a dedicated set, only on its locked press for its customer). Every cycle a set is in a press adds one to its counter; health = life left ÷ life limit. The planner only gives a set work its health still allows, books refurbishment before it wears out, and sends a worn idle set early when class-A work of its texture is waiting — one set of a texture at a time, so the texture keeps running.
