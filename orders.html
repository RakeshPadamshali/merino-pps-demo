Stateless balance-to-produce: BTP is never stored — it is recomputed from the order, the stock allocated to it and the good sheets already pressed every time the plan runs. Cancel an order, downgrade pressed sheets or record a customer rejection below and the balance, the plan and every page follow without anyone re-entering a requirement. Make-to-stock SKUs are served from free finished stock first.
