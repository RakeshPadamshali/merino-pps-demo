Show Paper Search
Paper gates the press. A line is only loaded when its decor paper is in stock — one sheet of paper per laminate face — from the stock at the as-of or a PO's ETA. When paper runs short the planner presses other work and comes back to the line when the PO lands; tight means stock and POs in the horizon cover the plan with less than 10% to spare.
