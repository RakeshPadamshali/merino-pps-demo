How plan and actual are compared

Actual is what the presses did — every cycle, its load, mould sets and print marks — with the breakdowns, cycle overruns and QC downgrades that happened.

Plan is the same loads at standard cycle time: the sheets each press would have made that day with no breakdown, no overrun and no downgrade (each press's own sheets per standard minute that day × the minutes lost, plus the sheets downgraded).

So every sheet of the gap has a recorded reason. Changeover time is planned, not a loss — it is where the Objective Studio and the Colour & Changeover page win time back.

In production the actuals come from press confirmations (PLC cycle counters and shift reports); here they are generated with the dataset.
