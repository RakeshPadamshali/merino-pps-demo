Press load —
Each row is one daylight: one texture from one mould set (8 plates for thin/standard HPL, 4 / 2 / 1 for compact as the books get thicker). Each cell is one plate, coloured by the decor it presses, with its sheet count — a single-sided plate carries 2 laminates, a double-sided 1, and where two books fit the bed (7 ft on the 14 / 16 ft beds, 8 ft on the 16 ft bed) the plate carries two books side by side. Every daylight in the cycle shares one cure profile, so every sheet is in one cure band; the load keeps its distinct thicknesses at or under the target. Click a plate for why it is here.
