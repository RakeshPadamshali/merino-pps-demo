Capacity at daylight × plate × cycle level — the worked example
Sheets a cycle = daylights × moulds per daylight (set by the cure band) × laminates per mould (2 single-sided, 1 double-sided) × books per plate (2 when two books fit the bed length). Cycles a day = 22 running hours × 90 % efficiency ÷ press minutes. Changeovers and cleaning passes come on top — the ATP quote below includes them.

Press × cure band capacity (single-sided, one book per plate)
Capacity is never a flat machine-hours number: a thin HPL load and a 25 mm compact load on the same press differ by a factor of forty in sheets per day, a double-sided sheet halves the plate yield, and on the 16 ft press two 8 ft books share a plate. Available-to-promise uses exactly these numbers.
