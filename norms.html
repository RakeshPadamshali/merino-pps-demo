How a norm works. Each make-to-stock SKU has a norm (the buffer target in sheets). Dealers draw from stock every day; a dealer order for the SKU is served from free stock first. When stock falls below two thirds of the norm (yellow) the planner raises a replenishment to bring it back to the norm; the weighted plan scores it by how deep into the buffer the SKU is (black and red first) and fills it after export and committed orders — Merino today fills it strictly last.
